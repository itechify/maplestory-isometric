## 1. Structure

The layout has three parts:
- A long, low **lawn** (G1) on the west.
- Three 60 px steps up to the **door terrace** (G4) on the east. The **exit door is set into the face of a grassy hill block** (P02) there.
- Above, a **staircase of garden platforms** zig-zags up from both sides to two top terraces.

The ground uses the 60 px grid starting at 515. **All platforms sit 30 px off that grid** (y 305, 185, 125 and so on). Heights below are given in H = 60 px measured from G1, so platforms land on half-steps.

### 1.3 Reachability (approximate rules as in the 1000000 brief [memory])

- Everything is reachable.
- **From the door (G4):**
  - The lawn is 3 hops (walk down the steps).
  - The top terrace P14 is 10 hops: ladder 2598 to P02, then P04, P06, P07, P08, P09, P10, P11, P12, P14.
  - P13 is 11 hops (drop from P14).
- **From the lawn:** ladder 1512 to P01, rope 1563 to P03, then P05 and P07, which joins the main stair.
- So **one continuous zig-zag stair** runs from the east door up through the centre to the top, and a second short stair joins it from the west lawn.
- [V40] adds a floater at 1896..2064@305 and ropes at x 1966, 2380 (P04 to G3), 2485 (P13 down) and 1324 (P10 to P03). Do not use for v8.

## 5. What makes this map recognizable

- From the data:
  - **A garden, not a forest.** It is compact (1878 px wide) and full of **berry hedges, clover and flower beds**.
  - **A hobbit-style wooden door in a grassy hill** is both the entrance and the exit.
  - A neat **zig-zag stair of stepping stones and terraces** climbs to a top terrace.
  - **A swarm of Shrooms** (28) fills it.
  - It has the lowest vertical span of the four maps briefed here: 870 px.
- [memory] This was the quick early grind spot for Shrooms right next to the Field's hollow.

## 6. 3D translation [proposal]

Concept: **a walled terrace garden behind a hill door.** H = 60 px measured from the lawn (G1). Platforms sit on half-steps.

1. **Door Terrace (G4, +3 H)** sits at the east (screen-right). The player arrives here, in front of a **round wooden door set into a grassy hillock (P02)**. The hillock is about 350 px wide and 2.5 H high, with a flower bed of clover and orange flowers on top. A short wooden ladder (x 2598) is fixed to its face beside the door.
   - The 6 spawn points cluster on this terrace and the steps.
   - The terrace descends west by **three wide garden steps (G3, G2)** to the lawn.
2. **Lawn (G1, 0 H)** is a long garden lawn running west, about 1260 px, with berry hedges along its back edge, 4 white-trunk trees and big bushes. It is the main Shroom field (8 Shrooms and 3 Orange Mushrooms).
3. **West stair**: from the lawn, a wooden ladder (x 1512) climbs to a small stepping platform (P01, +3.5 H). A rope or vine (x 1563) climbs from there to the **West Terrace (P03, +6.5 H)**, a raised bed with a berry hedge.
4. **East stair**: from the door-hill top (P02, +5.5 H), stepping stones (P04, then P06) zig-zag north-west to the **Central Terrace (P07, +8.5 H)**. The west stair joins here through P05.
5. **Upper stair**: from P07, stones P08 and P09 lead west to the **Upper-West Terrace (P10, +11.5 H)**, a clover bed. Stones P11 and P12 then lead back east to the **Top Terrace (P14, +14.5 H)**, with berry hedges and clover.
6. The **Top-Right Terrace (P13, +13.5 H)** is one step down from P14, at the back-right corner, above the door hill. It has a big bush and purple flowers. It is a dead end.
7. **Depth**: wind the stair **around a central garden mound**, so the stepping stones (168 px) climb a hill rather than float. Use the half-step offset (platforms sit 30 px off the ground grid) as low retaining walls between beds. **[proposal]** Make some stepping stones giant mushroom caps, so the garden visually earns its name (the v8 WZ has no mushroom props here).
