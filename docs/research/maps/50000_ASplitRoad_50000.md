## 1. Structure

### 1.1 Core reading
This map is a **two-level road**:
- The **lower road** T13 runs 2970 px across almost the whole map at y 365.
- Behind it rise two raised earth masses:
  - the **mesa** (x 456..1884, up to y −55);
  - the **embankment / upper road** T4 (x 1884..3234), which climbs a ramp to y 5 and runs to the east edge.
- Both roads reach the east edge, and **each has its own exit**. That fork is the "split". In 2D the lower road passes *in front of* the mesa and the embankment (it runs under them in x), and the render shows it as a continuous strip along the bottom.

## 5. What makes this map recognizable
- **Data + [minimap]:**
  - **The fork:** a lower valley road and an upper embankment road run side by side and leave through two separate exits at the east end, **each marked by a signpost**. The lower road leads to Southperry, the upper road to the fields west of Amherst.
  - A central **mesa** with a big yellow tree.
  - Maria waiting on a small flowered platform near the start, reached by a short ladder.
  - A pyramid of three floating blocks over the mesa's east shoulder.
  - First Shrooms.
- **[memory]:** the name alone ("A Split Road") is the landmark: it is where players choose between heading to the port town or toward Amherst.

## 6. [proposal] 3D translation

**Concept: the road forks in plan, not only in height.** The spine of this map is a **Y-shaped road**.

**Shared trunk (west)**
- Entry ground T11 (798 px, with the trees) descends two 60 px steps (T12) onto the road.
- **Maria's platform T5** becomes a small raised flower terrace or rock beside the trunk. It is 180 px up a short ladder (x77) and 258 px long.

**Lower branch (front)**
- **T13** continues straight east along the **front** edge of the map as a 2970 px valley road, passing **in front of** the mesa and the embankment.
- It ends at **east01 → Southperry** (front-right corner), with the lower signpost.
- Most spawns (27) live here.

**Upper branch (back)**
- After the mesa, the **embankment T4** peels off toward the **back** (north-east). It runs 500 px at +180 over the lower road, then climbs a **270 px ramp** (180 px rise) to a 580 px crest at +360.
- It ends at **east00 → The Field West of Amherst** (back-right corner), with the upper signpost.
- In plan the two branches diverge, so the player sees both destinations and chooses.

**Mesa (middle-back, between trunk and fork)**
- A raised plateau with stepped west terraces T8 (+240) → T6 (+300) → T3 (+360, then a 90 px ramp to +420 over the lower road).
- Its east shoulder steps down twice by 120 px (T7) onto the upper road. The mesa therefore doubles as a "high route" from the trunk to the upper branch.
- The big yellow tree goes on top.
- **The trio T1/T2/T0** becomes a pile of boulders or a rock outcrop on the shoulder (60 px steps). It is a vantage point; v22 puts Mai there.

**Connections**
- Ladder x2095 and the T10 rope pair become ladders or rope-ways up the embankment's front face.
- Ropes x502 and x1397/x1503 become climbs up the mesa face.
- T9 and T10 become rock ledges half-way up the faces.

**Proportions:** 3858 × 540 px in 2D, with 4 bands. Because the map is so wide and flat, use the depth for the fork. Separating the two branches by roughly the height difference between the roads (180..360 px) makes the split readable from the iso camera.
