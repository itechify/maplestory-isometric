## 1. Structure

### 1.1 Core reading
Southperry is a **harbour town in five zones**, running west → east:
1. **West town ground** (T29, y 287): the arrival from A Split Road, with a large domed house.
2. A **240 px cliff** at x 302 drops into a **sunken market street** (T34, y 527 rising to 407): shops, the Armor Store door and Mystic Door spots.
3. An **upper terrace** (T12/T10, y −133/−193) floats high above the street, with crates, a gazebo and Biggs. It is reached by a long ladder (west) or a stair of stone slabs (east).
4. At the street's east end a **lighthouse** stands on a rock mound (T28). A **grand stone staircase** (7 rises × 60 px) climbs to the **quay** (T20, y −13).
5. A **docked sailing ship**: gangplank, gunwale, deck, and a mast climbable to two yards and a masthead.

## 4. Visual reference
**Terrain tiles [render]:** everything walkable is **pale beige and cream irregular flagstone** masonry: light stones of varying size with grey mortar, like whitewashed cobblestone or sandstone. The cliffs, steps, terrace slabs and quay all use the same set. The ship is rendered in full: a brown wooden hull with gold and green trim, three masts, cream square sails, a bowsprit pointing east, and the stern on the west.

**Objects [minimap]** (the render omits most of them):
- **West town:** a **large white house with a blue dome**, plus a white arch or wing on its east side (x ≈ −380..190). A small crate (T26). A wooden signboard at the foot of the west cliff (x ≈ 470..580, street level).
- **Street:**
  - The **Armor Store**: a white house with a blue slanted roof and a chimney (x ≈ 530..946; its door is in00 at x 699).
  - A **second white house with a blue dome** (x ≈ 1040..1380).
  - **Coloured cloth banners or market racks** in red, green and pink (x ≈ 1280..1440).
- **Upper terrace:** blue crates (x ≈ 580..690), a green bush, and a **wooden gazebo/pavilion** (x ≈ 1026..1256, roof up to y ≈ −409).
- **Lighthouse:** a **white tower with a blue dome cap** (x ≈ 1826..2044, top y ≈ −323) on a rock-and-timber base, with a small blue-roofed shed (x ≈ 1980..2216).
- **Quay [render + minimap]:** two **unidentified decorative objects**:
  - a figure of a blonde woman in a white dress with a dark aura (x ≈ 3650..3750);
  - an orange ring-shaped object with white cloud puffs, possibly a life-buoy (x ≈ 3790..3900).

  Shrubs stand at the quay's east end.

**Likely missing from the render [memory]:**

## 6. [proposal] 3D translation

**Concept: a harbour amphitheatre.** The 2D west → east line becomes a descent and then a climb around a sunken plaza, ending at a quay with the ship moored along the **back** (sea side), so its tall masts rise behind the play space rather than in front of the camera.

**Zone A: west headland (front-left, +240 over the street)**
- **T29** becomes a raised town square where the road from A Split Road arrives (west00 at its far west).
- The **domed house** stands at the back of the square. **T21** (Paul and the h001 spot) becomes the house's balcony: visible but not reachable, as in the data.
- **The 240 px cliff edge:**
  - Ropes x364/x442 become two rope descents from the ledge **T30**, which sits across a 26 px gap on the cliff lip.
  - **Option:** in 3D, a carved stair down the cliff would make the town friendlier. If added, keep the ropes as well for fidelity.

**Zone B: sunken market plaza (front-centre)**
- **T34** becomes a long plaza: 1138 px at the low level, then a 180 px ramp up 120 px, then 688 px at the upper level.
- It is lined on its **back side** by the Armor Store (door in00), the second domed house and the banner racks.
- Bari stands in the plaza; the Mystic Door spots are spread along it.

**Zone C: high terrace (back-centre)**
- **T12/T10** becomes a belvedere 660..720 px above the plaza floor, running along the back of the plaza above the shop roofs. It has the gazebo, the blue crates and Biggs.
- **West access:** the long ladder x405 (397 px) from the cliff ledge.
- **East access:** ladder x1649 from the plaza, then the **slab stair** T22 → T19 → T17 → T13 (4 × 60 px floating stone slabs, which can become cantilevered stone steps on a wall), then a hop onto T10.

**Zone D: lighthouse and grand stair (front-right of the plaza)**
- **T28** becomes a rocky outcrop at the plaza's east end, with the lighthouse on it at the water's edge.
- The **grand staircase** (7 × 60 px rises, 90 px treads) climbs from the plaza east and up to the quay. It is the town's monumental axis.

**Zone E: quay and ship (back-right)**
- **T20** (1234 px) becomes a stone quay. Shanks stands at the gangplank foot (x ≈ 3100..3220).
- The ship is moored **along the back edge of the quay**, parallel to it:
  - gangplank T7 (204 px ramp to a landing);
  - gunwale ramp T5 onto the deck;
  - main deck T3 with a bow rising 170 px to the east;
  - quarter deck T4 at the stern (west), 45 px higher, backed by the 209 px stern wall.
- The **main mast** (x ≈ 2955) carries rope ladders (ratlines) up to the lower yard T2 (≈ 360 px up from the deck), the upper yard T1 (≈ 360 px more) and the crow's nest T0 (≈ 1130 px above the quay). This is an optional vertical landmark.
- The quay props (T14/T18 stack, the figure and ring objects, the shrubs) cluster at the east end.
