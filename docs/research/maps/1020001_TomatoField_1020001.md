## 5. What makes it recognizable
1. **Rows of tomato plants**: 72 of them, filling two sunken furrows (data + [visual]).
2. **Long leafy vines** of 326..448 px climbing to a **canopy of 17 small floating platforms** (data).
3. A calm **RestNPeace** BGM instead of FloralLife (data).
4. A secret feel: you reach it only through the hidden portal on the East Field's DANGER-sign ledge, and it has only one exit (data).
5. Gentle slopes instead of steps (data).

## 6. 3D translation [proposal]

### 6.1 Core idea
- A **walled-off farm field**. The two furrows become real **planted beds** whose tomato rows run **into depth** (north-south), so mobs walk between the rows.
- The hump becomes a grassy ridge between the beds, and the east plateau a raised orchard bank with the tree pair.
- The west bank holds the arrival point (`out00`) under the autumn trees. It should feel like the mouth of a hidden path: a hedge gap or tunnel matching the hidden entry on the East Field.
- The 17-platform canopy becomes a **trellis / pergola walkway**: wooden tomato-trellis decks and stake platforms 360..420 px above the beds, linked as a zig-zag canopy route.
  - Keep the 60 px row spacing and the 168 px deck size.
  - Interleave the decks in depth as well as height, so the canopy is a 3D lattice rather than a flat zig-zag.
- The five **vines** grow up trellis poles, one per zone. Bottoms hang about 30..61 px above the ground, so you jump to grab them.
- No mobs on the canopy (data). It is the safe overlook and a route across the field.
