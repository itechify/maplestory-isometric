# Map 1000004: "Snail Garden". Structural analysis and 3D translation brief

Machine-readable companion: `docs/research/maps/1000004.struct.json`. It uses the same tier ids as this brief.

- No tag: a fact taken from data. The source file or URL is given.

**Grid facts derived from the data**
- Every layer-1 platform top sits on a 60-px vertical grid, at y ≡ 35 (mod 60).
- Every floating platform is 168 + n·90 px wide. A 168-px platform is two 78-px end caps plus a 12-px joiner.
- Neighbouring platforms are separated by exactly 60 px vertically, and 12 px horizontally where they sit side by side.
- The layer-0 door block is offset by 30 px from that grid: its top is at y185.

### 1.3 Layering, gaps and walls
- **Ground band (y335..515)** is a stepped bowl. From the west it runs T21 y335 → 60-px riser → T22 y395 → riser → T23 y455 → riser → basin T25 y515. On the east side a 60-px riser climbs up to T24 y455. Every riser is a plain vertical wall of 60 px.
- **The door block T13** stands on the west half of T21. It is a 150-px earth cube, 348 px wide, with walls on both sides.
- **The low arch (y155..275)** is eight 168-px stones in a shallow arch over the basin:
  - T18 → T14 → T19/T15 → T16 → T12 (top, y155) → T17 → T20.
  - It starts one 60-px hop above T21's east end and ends on a ladder down to T24.
  - Its stones sit 240-300 px above the basin floor.
- **Mid row (y-25..-145):**
  - A west stair of three 168-px stones: T11 → T8 → T6.
  - Two long terraces: T9 (528 px) and T10 (438 px), linked through stone T7.
- **Upper row (y-205/-265):** T4 and T5 on the east. They are only reachable by the ladder from T10.
- **Top row (y-325..-445):** four islands, T1, T0, T2 and T3.
  - T1 and T0 are 192 px apart. T0 and T2 are also 192 px apart, and T0 is 60 px higher than both.
  - [memory] That is too far for a normal jump, so each top island is reached only by its own ropes.
  - T2 and T3 are joined by a 12-px gap with a 60-px step.
- **Vertical span:** the summit T0 is 960 px above the basin floor. That is 5 rows: ground, low arch, mid, upper and top.

### 1.4 Routes [inference]
- **Arrival:** 6 spawn points at x827-1276, y271-284 drop onto T21 next to the door block.
- **West climb:**
  1. Ladder x891 to the roof of the door block.
  2. Rope x1091 to T11.
  3. Then either the stair west (T8 → T6 → rope x1013 → vegetable island T1), or a hop east onto the central terrace T9.
- **Central:** from T9, rope x1322 goes to T1, rope x1634 goes to summit T0, and rope x1689 goes down into the low arch. A hop up to T7, then rope x1892, also reaches T0.
- **East:** drop from T7 to T10. Then ladder x2202 → T4 → drop to T5 → rope x2410 → T2 → step down to T3. Rope x2315 also drops from T10 into the arch at T12.
- **Low loop:** T21 → arch → ladder x2504 → T24 → down into the basin → back up the west risers to T21. Each riser is 60 px, which [inference] the design treats as jumpable.

- The same portal parses in v40.
- On the other side, `in03` is in The Field West of Amherst at (1908, 456), on that map's ground level, and links back to `1000004:out00`. Source: raw v8 WZ `https://maplestory.io/api/wz/GMS/8/Data/Map/Map/Map0/001000000.img/portal/6`, also recorded in `1000000.struct.json`.
- **No left or right edge exits.** The walls at x=771 and x=2919 run from the ground down to y=720, and there is no other non-spawn portal. This breaks the usual "exit at each end" pattern: the map is a dead-end pocket that you enter and leave through the same door.

## 5. What makes this map recognizable
- A **door set into an earth mound** at the west end. It is the only way in or out.
- A **stepped drop** into a **fenced sunken basin** that holds the most snails.
- An **arch of eight small stepping stones** curving over the basin.
- Four rows of grass islands, with the top row holding **literal garden beds**: carrots, small fruit bushes, strawberries and a sunflower clump. The name "Snail Garden" matches these props [inference].
- Only snails live here, in three colours. The music is the calm `RestNPeace`.
- [memory] I have no specific memory of this map beyond it being one of the snail side pockets west of Amherst.

## 6. 3D translation [proposal]

**Concept: a terraced kitchen garden in a hollow, entered through a hobbit-style door in a grassy mound.**
- MapleStory "up" becomes 3D up plus back. Each higher row of platforms steps back, away from the camera, and up.
- The retaining walls and cliff faces between rows therefore face the camera, and every climb happens on a visible face.
- The left-to-right order of the 2D map is kept as the main west-to-east axis along screen-left → screen-right.

**Tier mapping**
- **Ground band → front of the scene, lowest.**
  - T21 becomes the entrance terrace: a flat lawn.
  - T22 and T23 become two wide earth steps down into the basin. Each is a 60-px drop. Build them as a short flight of turf steps rather than walls, so the bowl reads as a dug-out garden.
  - T25 becomes the sunken vegetable plot: the largest flat area, fenced along its back edge (3 fence runs).
  - T24 becomes the east bank, one step up, with the yellow twin tree and the mixed tree as a grove.
- **Door block T13 → a grassy mound** on the west of the entrance terrace, about 150 px tall and about 350 px across.
  - The door is in its camera-facing side.
  - The ladder x891 becomes a short wooden ladder or steps up its side to the strawberry patch on the roof.
- **Low arch (8 stones) → a curved row of round stepping stones or cut stumps.** It swings out over the back of the sunken plot like a garden path on posts.
  - It starts beside the entrance terrace (one hop up) and lands on the east bank by a ladder.
  - Keep its stones small (168 px) and at their 60-px offsets. That is what makes it a hopping path.
- **Mid row → the first raised terrace wall** behind the plot:
  - T9 is the central terrace: a long bed with a big bush and an orange flower border.
  - T10 is the east terrace.
  - T11 → T8 → T6 becomes a three-step stone stair climbing west along the terrace wall.
- **Upper row (T4, T5) → two small stone landings** on the east, reached by the ladder from T10.
- **Top row → four hilltop garden beds**, the highest and furthest back:
  - T1: the vegetable bed (carrots and fruit bushes).
  - T0: the sunflower summit, the highest point.
  - T2: the purple flower bed.
  - T3: the carrot bed, one step lower than T2.
  - The 192-px gaps between beds become deep gaps or a ravine, so the beds stay rope-only, as in 2D.
- **Ropes → trellis ladders or rope ladders hanging down the terrace walls.** The 318-px rope x1634 becomes the tallest trellis and works as a visual landmark. The three ladders stay wooden ladders.

**Route through 3D space**
- The player starts on the entrance lawn, outside the door mound (front-left).
- The low loop runs down the steps into the sunken plot, across it, up onto the east bank, back along the stepping-stone arch, and back to the lawn.
- The vertical loops climb the terrace walls by trellises from front to back.
- Exits: the door is the only exit. Keep both the left and right ends closed with a hedge or a terrace wall. The 2D map has no edge exits.

**Spawn areas**
- The sunken plot plus the east bank is the main snail field: 13 of 47.
- The central and east mid terraces hold 11.
- Each top bed holds 4-5.
- The roof of the door mound holds 3.
- The entrance lawn holds 2, so a newly arrived player is not mobbed.

## 7. Version notes
- The v8 and v22 data are identical: footholds, ladders, mobs and portals.
- v40 has the same floors and mobs but adds a rope at x1969 (y217-473).
- v55 drops to 37 mobs, and v62 is a re-layout (only 23 of 115 floor footholds are shared). Do not use v55+ for MisoStory.

## 8. Open questions

- Should the 192-px gaps between the top islands stay impassable without ropes, as in 2D, or become plank bridges for 3D navigation comfort?
