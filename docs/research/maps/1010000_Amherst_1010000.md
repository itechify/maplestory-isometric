# Map 1010000: "Amherst". Structural analysis and 3D translation brief

Machine-readable companion: `docs/research/maps/1010000.struct.json` (tier ids T01..T14 below match it).

**Tags used in this brief**
- No tag: a fact taken from data. The source file or URL is given.
- **[visual]**: what a v8 object sprite or reference render shows. The object path is the fact; the description is my reading of the picture.
- **[inference]**: my reading of the data. The data does not state it.
- **[memory]**: something I remember about the game. No source was checked.
- **[proposal]**: a design suggestion for MisoStory.

**Sources**
- `docs/research/raw/gms_v8/1010000.json` (`https://maplestory.io/api/GMS/8/map/1010000`): footholds, ladders/ropes, NPCs, map info. The parsed `portals[]` there has 5 `null` entries.
- Raw GMS v8 WZ tree, which has every portal: `https://maplestory.io/api/wz/GMS/8/Data/Map/Map/Map0/001010000.img/portal/<n>/{pn,pt,x,y,tm,tn}`. The same tree gives `life/<n>`, `back/<n>`, `info/*` and the object lists `<layer 0..7>/obj/<n>/{oS,l0,l1,l2,x,y,z,f}`.
- Object sizes (used for the landmark bboxes): `https://maplestory.io/api/wz/GMS/8/Data/Map/Obj/<oS>.img/<l0>/<l1>/<l2>/0`, using the PNG width/height and `/origin`. I viewed the sprites in a temp folder only and did not copy them into the project.
- NPC names: `https://maplestory.io/api/GMS/8/npc/{12000,12100,12101,10000}`.
- Reference renders, viewed but not copied: `docs/research/ref_renders/v8_1010000_render.png` and `v8_1010000_minimap.png`.
- Version check: `docs/research/raw/gms_v22/1010000.json`. Footholds, ladders and portals match v8. The NPC list differs (see 3.2).

Every walkable foothold is assigned to one tier. The map has no ceilings (one-way undersides).

**Walls, cliffs and layering**
- Map edges: x=-398 and x=3278, both running y 274..480.
- West cliff of the plateau: x=-83, y -176..270, 446 px tall.
  - The plateau (x -83..893) lies directly above the west street (x -398..720).
  - In 2D the two levels overlap in x, one stacked on the other.
- Cliff under the ladders: x=1073, y -116..150.
- The four 60 px steps are at x=862 (up), 893 (up, on top), 2018 (down) and 2108 (down).
- There are no pits or gaps on the ground chain. You can walk from x=-398 to x=3278. The only blocks are the 60 px steps, which are jump-ups.
  - [memory] In classic level design, 60 px is a standard single-jump step.

### 1.3 Route summary [inference]

1. West street T01.
2. Ramp T02.
3. Jump 60 px up to the middle terrace T04.
4. Climb the ladder or rope (266 px) to T11.
5. Jump 60 px up to the upper plateau T12.

Going east from T04 you drop two 60 px steps (T05, T06) to the east street. The floating ledges T08→T09→T10 form an optional side climb above the east step.

## 5. What makes Amherst recognizable

1. **The cobblestone town on three levels.**
   - Street, a raised middle terrace and a high walled plateau.
   - The only way up the plateau is the paired rope and ladder (data).
2. **Five distinct mushroom houses, each with its own cap colour.**
   - Yellow, red tower, yellow + thatch, lime and orange-spotted, plus the giant tree house (data + [visual]).
   - The Weapon Store sits on top of the cliff and the Department Store on the terrace.
3. **East-end fairground props.**
   - Mushroom face cut-outs, a banner and the tree house next to the exit (data + [visual]).
4. **The red mailbox at the west gate.**
   - Plus the beginner NPCs: Rain's movement tips by the gate, and Lucas on the terrace (data).
5. `FloralLife` BGM and the grassySoil sky backdrop (data).
   - [memory] Amherst is the "second town" a beginner reaches on the classic Maple Island.

## 6. 3D translation [proposal]

### 6.1 Core idea
- The 2D data already contains a depth hint. **The upper plateau sits directly above the west street** (x -83..893 over x -398..720).
- In 3D, put the plateau **behind** the west street as a high back terrace held up by a cobblestone retaining wall about 450 px tall. Do not stack it overhead.
- The middle terrace becomes the town **plaza**, east of the street. The east street then turns toward the camera to reach the tree-house exit.
- The town reads as an **L/U-shaped walk around a raised core**, not as a strip.

### 6.2 Tier by tier
- **T01 west street (1118 px)**
  - The front lane from the west gate (`west00`) to the plaza ramp. The mailbox stands at the gate.
  - The yellow-capped house and the red tower line the lane's back edge, against the retaining wall.
  - Rain stands by the gate and Pio by the tower.
  - The Mystic Door points (x 169..621) span the lane's middle.
- **T02 + T03**
  - A real cobblestone ramp, 90 px run and 60 px rise, then one 60 px step. Together they form a stepped embankment up to the plaza.
- **T04 plaza (1156 px)**
  - The Department Store faces the plaza. The rainbow-spring prop is a plaza feature, and Lucas stands under the tree grove at the plaza's far side.
  - The plaza's back edge is the 270 px cliff of the upper terrace, with the **rope + ladder pair** (74 px apart) on its face.
- **T11 + T12 upper terrace (back)**
  - A small landing at the ladder top (T11), then a 60 px step onto the plateau.
  - The Weapon Store sits on the plateau, framed by its two trees, with Mai and the barrels.
  - The plateau's front edge looks down 450 px onto the west street. That edge is the town's main vista.
  - Keep the plateau **only** reachable by the ladder and rope, as in the data.
- **T05 + T06 east street (1170 px)**
  - Two 60 px steps down from the plaza.
  - The lane curves toward the camera past the orange Townstreet house, the cut-out boards and the banner.
  - It ends at the giant tree house, with `east00` at its roots.
  - Keep the 223 px of street beyond the exit as a dead-end nook behind the tree.
- **T08/T09/T10**
  - A small wooden **lookout tower** at the plaza's east corner, over the east steps.
  - T08 is the low platform, then rope x=2072 (176 px), then deck T09, then a 60 px jump to the roof T10.
  - It is an optional climb with a view over both streets.
- **Barrel tops** T07, T13, T14: standable props, 50 px tall.

### 6.3 Portals in 3D
- `west00`: a gate arch at the west end of the front lane.
- `in00`: the Weapon Store door on the back terrace.
- `in02`: the Department Store door on the plaza.
- `in01`: the orange house door on the east street.
- `east00`: the tree-house roots at the south-east end.
