## 1. Structure

The map has three structural parts:
- (a) a **flat forest floor** across the whole width;
- (b) a **zig-zag tower** of 168 px floaters climbing from bottom to top in the left-centre (x -174..354), with three side ledges branching west;
- (c) a **right-hand complex**: a 4-step hill on the floor, a column of three floaters, the arrival ledge (P14), and the huge top ledge (P24) with three ropes hanging off it.

Every y value is on a 60 px grid.

### 1.3 Reachability (60 px jump, about 50 px up-jump gap, about 100 px rope grab [memory, approximate])

- Every tier can be reached. Nothing is unreachable.
- **From the arrival portal (P14):**
  - P24 is 1 hop (rope 981) and the summit P25 is 4 hops (P24, drop to P22, P23, P25).
  - The floor is 1 hop (drop).
- **From the floor back to the exit:** 6 hops. Ladder 1142 to P02d, step down to P02c, rope 853 to P08, then P10, P12, P14.
  - The alternative is the tower: rope -406 to P01, then up through P03 ... P11, drop to P10, then P12 and P14.
- The tower climb from G1 to the summit is about 10 hops. Every move is a single 60 px jump, with one rope at the start.
- [V40] adds ropes at x -54 (P03 to floor), -472 (P19 to P06), 290 (P22 to P11), 359 and 445 (to a new v40 platform at 276..444@365). Do not use them for v8.

## 5. What makes this map recognizable

- From the data:
  - **A tall, narrow "climbing tower"** of 14 tiny 168 px floaters (plus the P11 ledge) zig-zagging up the left-centre, each one jump apart.
  - **The arrival is mid-air** on a right-side ledge, from which you look down onto an open forest floor.
  - **A huge top ledge** with three ropes hanging down like curtains.
  - A **4-step hill** in the bottom-right corner with a ladder.
  - Autumn-coloured white-trunk trees everywhere.
  - Orange Mushrooms patrol the floor.
- [memory] Players used it as an early, quiet training spot: easy snails on the ledges, and a little more danger on the floor.

## 6. 3D translation [proposal]

Concept: **a small forest glade wrapped around one giant tree.** One 60 px step = 1 tier H. Screen-left = west.

1. **Glade floor (G1)** is a flat, roughly oval clearing about 2058 px across, at tier 0. It is ringed by a dense wall of white-trunk autumn trees, which replaces the solid edge walls. The floor is the "danger ring" where the Orange Mushrooms and Shrooms roam.
2. **The Giant Tree (the tower P03 to P23)** stands left of centre. The zig-zag floaters become **shelf fungi or stump steps spiralling around a huge white trunk**. The tower is 528 px wide. It rises 780 px (13 H) from P03 to P23, and 14 H to the crown P25, so a spiral gives it real depth instead of a flat ladder. Keep one 1 H step per platform.
   - Rope -406 to P01 becomes a vine on the trunk's west root that leads to the first shelf (P01, +4 H).
   - P11 (+10 H) is a broad bracket ledge on the trunk's east side, facing the glade.
3. **West branches (P06 at +8 H, P19 at +15 H)** are long branch-walks reaching back-left from the trunk toward the west edge. Dead ends, with snail spawns.
4. **Crown (P25, +19 H)** is the tree's crown platform: a lookout with bushes and the summit spawn.
5. **Canopy Log (P24, +18 H)** is a long fallen log or thick bough, 798 px, running from near the crown east toward the right side, behind and above the arrival ledge. **Three vines hang from it** (x 811, 890, 981). One lands on the arrival ledge. The other two end in mid-air above P12 and are one-way drops.
6. **Arrival ledge (P14, +11 H)** is a grassy cliff shelf on the east edge, facing west over the glade. The out00 portal sits at its centre, between two red-berry bushes. This is where the player spawns. Give it a view of the Giant Tree.
7. **East stepping stones (P08, P10, P12)** are three stumps descending from the arrival ledge's west end toward the hill. Rope 853 becomes a vine from P08 down to the hill.
8. **East Hill (P02a to P02d)** is a terraced knoll in the south-east (front-right) corner: 3 grassy steps of 1 H, then a 528 px top at +4 H with two trees. Ladder 1142 becomes a wooden ladder on its front face down to the glade floor.
